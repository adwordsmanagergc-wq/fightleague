Opening round · 10.10.2026

Thank you, fighters.

Sixteen fighters opened the league at Max Muay Thai Stadium on 10 October and gave Pattaya a night to remember. Eight won through to the semi-finals. Every one of them earned our respect.

Before the first bell

Wai Kru. Ram Muay.

ไหว้ครู รำมวย

Before the first bell, both fighters perform the same sacred ritual side by side. It honours their teachers, their camp and their family. This is what you will see.

  1. Into the ring

    Wearing the sacred mongkol, the fighter enters the ring.

  2. Sealing the ring

    A hand on the top rope and a prayer at every corner.

  3. Wai Kru

    Three bows: to the Buddha, parents and teachers.

  4. Ram Muay

    The dance. Every camp has its own moves.

  5. The mongkol comes off

    The trainer lifts the mongkol. The first bell rings.
