16 fighters confirmed for the IFL opening round

The 2026 IFL Muay Thai League opens on Saturday 10 October at Max Muay Thai Stadium, Pattaya, with eight men at 63kg and eight women at 51kg.

Eight bouts make up the opening round card. Every bout is three rounds of three minutes, and every winner moves on to the semi-finals on Saturday 21 November.

The card includes both fighters from the IFL-5 main event: Hamid Boughanem and Parsa Aminpour, known as Lord Parsa, met in a world title fight on 22 August. This time they are in the same league.

Doors open at 4:00 PM and the first fight starts at 4:00 PM. General admission tickets are ฿1,500 on Ticketmelon.
