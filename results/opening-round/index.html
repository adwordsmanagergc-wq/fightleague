Opening round · 10.10.2026

Thank you, fighters.

Saturday 10 October 2026, Max Muay Thai Stadium, Pattaya. Sixteen fighters opened the IFL Muay Thai League, and what a night they gave us.

Eight bouts. Seven went the full distance, and Parsa Aminpour scored the only stoppage of the night with a round-two TKO. From the Wai Kru to the final bell, every fighter came to win and left everything in the ring.

To all sixteen fighters, their trainers, corners and gyms: thank you. You set the standard for this league. To the eight who won through, congratulations, and we will see you at the semi-finals. To the eight whose league run ends here, you have our respect, and the door to the IFL stays open.

Thank you as well to everyone at Max Muay Thai Stadium, the officials, our partners, and every fan who came to Pattaya or watched live on YouTube.
