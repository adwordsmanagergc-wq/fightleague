About the league

Pattaya's
Muay Thai
league

The International Fighters League (IFL) runs Muay Thai fight nights at Max Muay Thai Stadium in Pattaya. In 2026 it becomes a league: three fight nights, one bracket, and ฿250,000 in prize money for the league winners.

The format

  1. 01

    Three rounds of three minutes.

  2. 02

    Men at 63kg and women at 51kg in the opening round.

  3. 03

    Win and advance. Lose and your league run ends.

  4. 04

    New weight divisions join from November.

League winner
฿250,000

Prize money for the league winners

  1. 10.10Opening round

    16 fighters, 8 bouts. Men 63kg and women 51kg.

  2. 21.11Semi-finals

    The opening round winners fight on. New weight divisions join the league.

  3. 26.12Finals

    Boxing Day. League winners take the prize money.

Before the first bell

Wai Kru. Ram Muay.

ไหว้ครู รำมวย

Before the first bell, both fighters perform the same sacred ritual side by side. It honours their teachers, their camp and their family. This is what you will see.

The ritual, step by step

  1. 01

    Into the ring

    ขึ้นเวที

    The fighter enters wearing the mongkol, the sacred headband. By tradition, men step over the top rope and women pass under the bottom rope, a sign of humility and respect.

  2. 02

    Sealing the ring

    เดินรอบเวที

    One hand on the top rope, the fighter walks the ring anti-clockwise and stops to pray at each corner. It seals the ring and keeps bad luck out.

  3. 03

    Wai Kru

    ไหว้ครู

    Kneeling on the canvas, the fighter bows three times in respect: to the Buddha, to their parents and to their teachers.

  4. 04

    Ram Muay

    รำมวย

    Then the dance. Every camp has its own moves, so a trained eye can tell where a fighter comes from. The Sarama music sets the pace.

  5. 05

    The mongkol comes off

    ถอดมงคล

    Back in the corner, the trainer lifts the mongkol from the fighter's head. The ritual is complete. The music speeds up and the first bell rings.

Three things to look for

  • มงคล

    Mongkol

    The sacred headband, often blessed at a temple. It is worn on the head, so it should never touch the floor.

  • ประเจียด

    Prajiad

    Cords tied around the upper arms for luck and protection.

  • สะระหม่า

    Sarama

    The live ring music of Thai oboe, drums and cymbals. Slow for the ritual, faster once the fight starts.
